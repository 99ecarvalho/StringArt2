STRING ART GENERATOR - BEST SETTINGS TO START FROM
==================================================
String Art Generator - (c) 2025-2026 Eduardo Correia <ecorreia@apliant.com.br>

These settings were found by running the generator on assets/einstein.jpg
with every combination of pins, opacity, line weight, minimum distance and
(for Radon) threshold, and ranking the results by the "Match to Target"
score. About 1,700 runs in total, confirmed at the default resolution
(radius 300).

Use them as a starting point for your own images: every image is different,
so experiment from here.


HOW TO USE
----------
1. Open index.html and upload assets/einstein.jpg (or your own image).
2. Leave Image Preparation at its defaults: Fit "Fill the circle (crop)",
   Zoom 1, positions 0, Contrast 0, Gamma 1.
3. Set the Parameters below. Settings not listed keep their defaults
   (Resolution 300, Pin 0 position 3 o'clock, Radon Angles 180).
4. Set String Iterations to 10000. It is only an upper limit: both
   algorithms stop by themselves when more string would not help.
5. Click Generate String Art.

WHICH ONE TO PICK
-----------------
- Best quality: the highest match score. Many nails and many lines: a long
  build by hand, fine for a robot.
- Balanced: almost the same quality with about a third fewer lines.
- Beginner: 200 nails and under 3,000 lines. The easiest to build by hand,
  and still clearly recognizable.

The rendered results of the "Best quality" settings are in this folder:
  einstein_greedy.png   Greedy, best quality
  einstein_radon.png    Radon Transform, best quality


WHAT THE SEARCH SHOWED
----------------------
- Many faint lines beat few dark ones: the score falls steadily as the
  opacity rises, from the best results at 5-10% to the worst at 30%.
- More pins help, but slowly: 200 pins reach about 88%, 500 pins about 91%.
- At resolution 300, a line weight of 2 px gives the best balance of quality
  and line count. At a lower resolution, use a proportionally thinner line
  (for example 1-1.5 px at resolution 200).
- Min Pin Distance hardly matters between 10 and 30.
- Radon Transform: keep the Darkness Threshold low (2-8). Higher values stop
  the generation too early on portraits.
- Greedy and Radon end up within about 0.5% of each other. Radon usually
  needs fewer lines for the same score and gives a little more contrast;
  Greedy renders smoother tones.


TIPS FOR YOUR OWN IMAGES
------------------------
- Fill the circle with the subject. Use Zoom and the position sliders until
  the face (or object) nearly touches the edge of the target preview.
- Flat-looking photo: raise Contrast to 20-40.
- Result too light or too dark overall: adjust Gamma (above 1 = darker
  midtones, more string; below 1 = lighter).
- The match score only compares runs made with the same image preparation.
  After changing contrast, gamma or the crop, compare the images by eye.
- Try a quick run at Resolution 200 to test ideas, then generate the final
  version at 300.
